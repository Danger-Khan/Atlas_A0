# Privacy Policy

*Plain-language summary. The machine-readable version is `policy.json`.*

- **No accounts, no keys, no tokens, no tracking.** AtlasA0 has no login, analytics, telemetry, crash
  reports or advertising. The author receives nothing.
- **What goes over the network.** Only the requests needed to draw a map: OpenStreetMap services (Nominatim,
  Overpass) and, if you choose them, tile servers (Esri, OpenTopoMap). These servers see your IP address and
  the area you ask for, as for any web request. Their own privacy policies apply.
- **What stays on your computer.** Maps, receptor tables, caches and settings are written only to the output
  and cache folders you can see in the app. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Your own files.** Layers, tables and rasters you add are read locally and are never sent anywhere.
- **Receptors / points of interest.** They come from OpenStreetMap, which is public data. Check the
  contents before sharing a file that contains named places.
- **Deleting data.** Delete the output and cache folders; there is nothing else.
- **Children.** AtlasA0 does not collect personal data from anyone.
