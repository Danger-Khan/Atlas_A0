# Code of Conduct

Be respectful. Harassment, hate, personal attacks and political flame wars have no place here.

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners in GIS or Python.
- Discuss work, not people. Give feedback that is specific and useful.
- Map boundaries and names can be sensitive; talk about data quality and sources, not politics.
- No spam, no advertising, no sharing of other people's private information.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these rules and may ban repeat offenders.
Report problems privately to yameenimaad@gmail.com. The author decides the outcome.

This code is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
